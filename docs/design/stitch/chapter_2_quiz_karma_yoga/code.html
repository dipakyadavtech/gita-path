Wisdom Note: Nishkama Karma

Krishna emphasizes that human agency lies exclusively in deliberate action (adhikāra), never in ultimate fruits (phaleṣu). Obsessing over outcomes breeds anxiety and paralysis; pouring intention wholly into the present duty generates mental stillness, clarity, and true autonomy.
